OUR APPROACH

Great measurements. Great music. Different questions.

A neutral measurement can be useful. So can knowing why you love a particular sound. We'll explain what equipment does, what the numbers can show, and where personal preference starts.

How we approach the evidence →
What sounds better to you?

Not every measurable difference is audible. Not every audible difference is an improvement. And not every preference needs to be defended as scientific fact.

Make informed choices, then enjoy the music.
