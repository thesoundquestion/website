The ABX Lab.
Can your ears tell?

You hear reference A, reference B, and a mystery X. Listen as many times as you like, then guess whether X is A or B. No account, no expensive equipment, no golden-ear credentials required.

Start with Training to learn the clues, then explore Challenge or Advanced. These are enjoyable, informal listening games and a community experiment, not scientific proof of what everybody can hear.

WHY TRY IT?

Listening is a skill. Curiosity is enough.

Training uses exaggerated mixing and loudness-matched mastering changes to help you learn what to listen for. Challenges are randomized. You can repeat any reference or mystery sample as many times as needed.

A high score isn't a certificate of hearing ability. A low score isn't a failure. Speakers, headphones, background noise, familiarity, volume and the particular music all matter. Community results are voluntary, anonymous, and unsupervised, so enjoy the comparisons rather than treating the numbers as a laboratory finding.

Want the story behind the sound?

Every comparison links back to its relevant full lesson, with the music, processing choices, explanations and source credits. Explore Mixing, Mastering, or MP3 versus Lossless before or after the test.
