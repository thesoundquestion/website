SITE INFORMATION

Privacy and
transparency.

A plain-language note about how this early website works.

What this site collects

The Sound Question does not require an account, analytics scripts, ad pixels, or marketing cookies. Visiting does not require sharing personal information. The optional community forms described below collect the words you choose to submit.

Lesson questions

Ask The Sound Question matches questions to prepared answers on your device. The live AI option is currently off.

Contact email

Writing to thesoundquestion@gmail.com opens your email provider rather than a form on this site. Emails and any information you choose to include are handled by the email services involved and retained as correspondence so we can respond. Do not send sensitive personal information or recordings you lack permission to share.

Hosting and external services

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website. Links to social media and other external websites take you to services with their own privacy practices. Donations open Ko-fi's external payment page, where that service handles the payment. Using our share or copy-link buttons does not load third-party scripts or send website analytics.

Community comments and recording suggestions

The ABX discussion and Curator's Listening Room offer optional anonymous forms. A recording suggestion contains the artist, recording title, selected Listening Room category and your short explanation (up to 280 characters). A comment contains its section and short text (up to 280 characters). We also store a random submission reference, submission and review timestamps, and whether an entry is pending, approved or rejected.

Submissions are saved in the site's database. Nothing is published automatically. A curator reviews entries before they appear in the public community lists. Approved entries may be shown with a Spotify search link generated by the site. Suggestions do not automatically join the official playlist. Do not include sensitive personal details, song lyrics you do not have permission to reproduce, or someone else's private information. We do not request or store a contributor account, email address or IP address in the submission table. To discourage spam, the site uses temporary limits based on your IP address.

Unreviewed submissions are scheduled for deletion after 90 days and rejected entries 30 days after review. Approved contributions remain visible until removed by the curator or on request. Save the reference shown after submission and email thesoundquestion@gmail.com if you want an entry removed. Do not submit material unless you are comfortable with the entry being published after review.

Equipment model recommendations

System Check may offer an optional form where you can suggest audio equipment for our preset library. We ask for the equipment category, manufacturer and model. A published manual link or technical notes are optional. Please do not enter your name, contact details, serial number or other personal information. We store a random submission reference, the submitted information, its review status and submission/review dates. We do not require an account or collect an email address in this form.

Suggestions are private while awaiting review and never become public presets automatically. We may check manufacturer documentation and independent measurements before adding a model to the site's curated specification files. Any published preset cites its own sources and can omit unverifiable claims. IP-based temporary rate limits help discourage spam; IP addresses are not stored in the gear-suggestion table. Pending recommendations are scheduled for deletion after 90 days if not reviewed, and rejected ones 30 days after rejection. Approved or incorporated records may be retained as part of the documentation trail. Save your submission reference and contact thesoundquestion@gmail.com to request removal.

Listening games and future community results

Current beta: Training, Challenge and Advanced run as practice games on your device. No practice answers or scores are sent to our score service or shared publicly.

When community scoring is eventually launched: a separate scoring service will check blind-test answers. If you choose to start a scored session after launch, the service will store a random session identifier, the comparison and difficulty, number of attempts and correct answers, a session-control hash, and whether you chose to share the completed score. No account, name, email or permanent listener identity is attached to the score. The service uses short-lived IP-based rate limiting to deter abuse, but does not store IP addresses in its scores database.

Sharing a completed result is optional and off by default. Only opted-in completed sessions enter the public statistics. Aggregates are not displayed for groups smaller than ten sessions. Incomplete sessions expire within two hours; completed private sessions are scheduled for deletion after one day, and opted-in results after 180 days. These are informal, unsupervised community results, not verified measurements of anyone's hearing.

Changes

We update this notice as the site evolves. Community scoring is not enabled in this beta. Normal lessons and all current practice games work without the scoring service.
