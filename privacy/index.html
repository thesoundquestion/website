SITE INFORMATION

Privacy and
transparency.

A plain-language note about how this early website works.

What this site collects

The Sound Question does not require an account, analytics scripts, ad pixels, or marketing cookies. Visiting does not require sharing personal information. The optional community forms described below collect the words you choose to submit.

Lesson questions

Ask The Sound Question matches questions to prepared answers on your device. The live AI option is currently off.

Contact email

Writing to thesoundquestion@gmail.com opens your email provider rather than a form on this site. Emails and any information you choose to include are handled by the email services involved and retained as correspondence so we can respond. Do not send sensitive personal information or recordings you lack permission to share.

Hosting and external services

The site is hosted through Cloudflare Pages. Like other hosting providers, Cloudflare may process technical request information for delivering and securing the website. Following a link to YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Threads, Ko-fi or an external sharing service takes you to that service, which has its own privacy practices. Ko-fi handles any optional financial support outside this website. The website's share controls do not load third-party scripts; sharing or copying a link does not itself send website analytics.

Community comments and recording suggestions

The ABX discussion and Curator's Listening Room offer optional anonymous forms. A recording suggestion contains the artist, recording title, selected Listening Room category and your short explanation (up to 280 characters). A comment contains its section and short text (up to 280 characters). We also store a random submission reference, submission and review timestamps, and whether an entry is pending, approved or rejected.

Submissions are saved to the site's Cloudflare D1 database. Nothing is published automatically. A curator reviews entries before they appear in the public community lists. Approved entries may be shown with a Spotify search link generated by the site. Suggestions do not automatically join the official playlist. Do not include sensitive personal details, song lyrics you do not have permission to reproduce, or someone else's private information. We do not request or store a contributor account, email address or IP address in the submission table. Cloudflare processes temporary IP-based request limits to discourage spam.

Unreviewed submissions are scheduled for deletion after 90 days and rejected entries 30 days after review. Approved contributions remain visible until removed by the curator or on request. Save the reference shown after submission and email thesoundquestion@gmail.com if you want an entry removed. Do not submit material unless you are comfortable with the entry being published after review.

Listening-lab community results

Training tests run on your device and do not submit a score. Challenge and Advanced sessions use a separate Cloudflare Worker and D1 database to check blind-test answers. When you choose to start a scored session, the service stores a random session identifier, the comparison and difficulty, number of attempts and correct answers, a session-control hash, and whether you chose to share the completed score. No account, name, email or permanent listener identity is attached to the score. The service uses short-lived IP-based rate limiting to deter abuse, but does not store IP addresses in its scores database.

Sharing a completed result is optional and off by default. Only opted-in completed sessions enter the public statistics. Aggregates are not displayed for groups smaller than ten sessions. Incomplete sessions expire within two hours; completed private sessions are scheduled for deletion after one day, and opted-in results after 180 days. Cloudflare may separately process technical requests for security and service operations. These are informal, unsupervised community results, not verified measurements of anyone's hearing.

Changes

We update this notice as the site evolves. Community scoring only becomes available once the server-side score service is enabled; normal lessons and Training do not require it.
