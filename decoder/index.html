MORE THAN GOOD VERSUS BAD

Different character isn't automatically a flaw.

Some listeners love bright, forward guitars. Others want a gentler balance. Some amplifier designs aim for minimal added sound; others deliberately introduce colour. These lessons distinguish accuracy, individual preference, and genuine performance limitations rather than declaring every non-flat line “bad.”
