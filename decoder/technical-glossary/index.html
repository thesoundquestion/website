A COMMON POINT OF CONFUSION

Harmonics versus overtones

The first harmonic is the fundamental, the main repeating frequency. The second harmonic is twice that frequency; the third is three times as fast. For a 100 Hz note, those are 100, 200 and 300 Hz.

An overtone is simply a component above the fundamental. In a harmonic series, the first overtone is the second harmonic. But not all overtones are harmonics: a bell can have higher tones that aren't exact multiples.

So “harmonic” describes a specific whole-number relationship; “overtone” describes position above the fundamental. Explore added harmonics in amplifiers →

ELECTRICITY WITHOUT THE EQUATIONS

One pump. Two taps. Different demands.

Imagine the amplifier as a pump. At the same pressure (voltage), a wider bathtub tap asks for more water (current) than a narrow sink tap. But opening the tap wider cannot increase what the pump is capable of supplying.
