What's happening with the bass?

Adding a subwoofer and actually high-passing the main speakers are different things. We do not give out free headroom.

A SUB OUT jack alone does not establish a high-pass to the mains. A verified filter requires the exact model, actual connections, firmware/settings and evidence.

How to read this preview

Facts, not vibes

Equipment preset claims link to their source where available. Sensitivity bases, load ratings, peak versus continuous output, and frequency-response tolerances matter.

Unknown is not a zero

Unmeasured directivity, clean bass output, room behaviour or used pricing is neither a failing score nor a hidden advantage.

No borrowed measurement database

Independent reviews are valuable links. We do not scrape, digitize or redistribute restricted measurements into a commercial-capable ranking system. Read Erin's measurement-data policy.
