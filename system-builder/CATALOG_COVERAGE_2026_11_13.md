**Release policy:** Do not block the Builder launch solely on catalogue parity. Supplemental identities appear in the Builder's own model menus; verified values can be entered manually in System Check. Never turn headline maximum amplifier wattage into continuous clean output, assume a missing impedance minimum, compare incompatible sensitivity methods, or claim power-handling standards without documentation. A separate, appropriate calculation is required for powered PA and headphones. Builder's dated price references expire after 30 days rather than masquerade as live or representative market prices.
