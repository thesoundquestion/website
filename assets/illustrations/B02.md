# B02 — Driver roles and construction

Revision 1.0 · 2026-11-11. The editable SVG artwork is also the vector print master. Captions, role labels and alt text live in the [speaker-driver lesson](/decoder/speaker-drivers/).

- **DRV-01** is a side cutaway of a generic sealed passive two-way loudspeaker. It shows the two drivers, cabinet, crossover and simplified routing. The terminals, component values, internal damping and crossover slopes are outside the scope of this construction illustration.
- **DRV-04** uses four comparable front elevations. The 2.5-way example shares bass across two woofers, with one continuing into the midrange. The three-way example assigns one driver to each of three principal roles. Actual crossover regions overlap and driver geometry, count, cabinet size and band assignment vary by design.

The drawings describe example architectures. [Dynaudio explains the 2.5-way design](https://dynaudio.com/magazine/2025/april/what-is-a-2-5-way-speaker-ask-the-expert). The [KEF Reference technical paper](https://assets.kef.com/pdf_doc/REF/REF-White-Paper-201219-LR.pdf) documents the design trade-offs behind one three-way implementation.
