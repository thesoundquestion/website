**Important scope:** These are horizontal relative off-axis contour plots of sound-pressure level as a function of frequency and direction. They cannot be reinterpreted as absolute frequency response or an on-axis vs listening-window overlay. Compare their patterns carefully; they do not demonstrate which speaker has greater bass extension, wider absolute frequency-response flatness or higher sound pressure at the central seat.
