Budget, mid-priced and premium refer only to typical new-retail expense among comparable equipment types in the selected region. A category is shown only when enough dated retailer evidence exists. Unknown is not “budget,” and more expensive does not mean better.

Regional labels are shown only where dated, comparable retailer evidence exists. For newly added equipment categories, no price category is inferred from a missing sample.

These numbers describe particular products, but they are not sound-quality scores. For instance, a headphone's claimed frequency range says little about how evenly it plays bass and treble unless the maker also states a level tolerance and test method. A missing or unchecked figure appears as “—” rather than an invented value.

Verification standards: source audit, known manufacturer conflicts and unverified models. A checked manufacturer specification is still not an independent bench measurement.
