# Speaker power handling provenance, 2026-11-11

50 of 59 speaker presets now include model-specific published speaker-power information (including explicitly labelled amplifier recommendations and secondary vintage transcriptions). Definitions are kept separate. Each augmented preset in `system-check/gear.js` carries its source URL, original-spec description and numeric values.

Field meanings:
- `speakerPowerContinuous`: **explicitly** published speaker continuous handling. Compare to long-term / average electrical demand; a peak beyond it is not automatic damage.
- `speakerPowerNominal`: manufacturer's **nominal input/handling**, whose time and test convention may differ from continuous. Compare as an advisory only.
- `speakerPowerPeak`: **explicit** short-peak or peak handling. Compare to short-peak demand, without implying a guaranteed safe duration.
- `speakerPowerMaxInput`: published maximum input with unclear time duration. Compare peak and average as a **caution**, never assume continuous or 1-second peak duration.
- `speakerRecommendedAmpMax`: **maximum of recommended amplifier power range**, which is NOT a speaker handling limit. Show only informationally.
- `speakerPowerSource`/`speakerPowerNote`: model-specific source and original label/caveat.

Manufacturer spec discrepancies: original ELAC Debut 3 owner manual `https://w.elac.com/download/dc53/debut-3-owners-manual_1.0.0.pdf` specifies DB53 nominal/peak 50/70 W; DB63 60/80; DF63 100/150. Its promotional overview includes conflicting single power values. Manual numbers are preferred and discrepancy disclosed. Yamaha NS-6490 Canadian listing nominal impedance 6 ohms differs from US page listing 8 ohms; the Canadian preset remains 6-ohm.

Important: Paradigm Phantom v1 and Titan v3 have qualified secondary transcriptions of model-specific published maximum input power and amplifier recommendations; these are labelled as secondary rather than manufacturer-verified measurements. They currently remain unknown. Do not convert published amplifier recommendations (KEF, DALI, Q Acoustics, JBL, Wharfedale) into speaker limits. Do not treat peak-only Cerwin-Vega values as continuous. All comparison wattages are nominal-resistive estimates and do not model bass excursion, thermal limits, impedance phase, clipping, equalizer boosts or the speaker's individual driver power distribution.
