# AlbumDB permission status — public-safe summary

**As of 2026-11-16:** A November 15 request specified public streaming of The Sound Question's independently edited and mastered educational excerpts, including the compositions and recordings. On November 16, Dr Thomas McKenzie approved that proposed use, requested citation of both the paper and artist, and offered to share completed video demonstrations. The complete source conversation was forwarded to the project mailbox and remains the authoritative reference. Do not quote private email correspondence in public pages.

## Publication guardrails

- Credit Tom McKenzie, the particular song and album, AlbumDB creators, dataset and CC BY 4.0 licence, and the research paper: https://aes.org/publications/elibrary/elibrary-page/?id=23185 (AES Convention 160, 2026, Paper 10278).
- Label independently modified examples clearly; no claim of official artist masters or endorsement.
- Permission is grounded in described **educational public streaming**. Do not infer rights to redistribute raw stems standalone, sell recordings, sublicense them or use them in new unrelated commercial projects.
- Keep final browser/audio testing and owner-approved deployment as separate gates.
