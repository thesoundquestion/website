Recommend your gear.

Can't find your amplifier, speakers, headphones, DAC or other audio equipment in our tools? Tell us the brand and exact model. We'll use your suggestion as a starting point for researching its specifications.

The more verified equipment we can add, the easier it gets for everyone to check compatibility, compare models and explore system options without searching through manuals on their own.

What happens to your recommendation?

  1. You tell us the model.Just the manufacturer and model number are enough to begin. The other details are optional, but can help us find the right version.
  2. We check the specifications.We look for original manuals, manufacturer documentation and credible independent measurements. Anything we can't confirm stays unknown.
  3. Verified models join the presets.Approved specifications can then help other visitors using System Check, Compare My Gear and System Builder.

Submitting a model doesn't automatically add it, prove any claims, or guarantee a product recommendation. We won't publish personal information from submissions.
