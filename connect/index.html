Get in touch

Questions, corrections and recording contributions

Have a listening-test idea, a question you want explained or feedback about the site? Musicians, engineers and researchers can also offer recordings they have permission to share for educational use.

Email a question or idea

Only offer materials you own or are authorized to share. Describe the material and permissions before sending large files.

Audio businesses and equipment makers

We welcome inquiries about equipment loans, demonstration units, review samples and technical information that help explain how audio equipment works.

Email a business inquiry

Please include the product, where it would ship from and whether it is a temporary loan or a product to keep. We will discuss shipping, return terms and permissions before accepting anything. An offer does not guarantee coverage, a favourable assessment or editorial approval. Loans, gifts and paid relationships will be disclosed where relevant, and any findings remain independent.

We use email rather than a contact form.
