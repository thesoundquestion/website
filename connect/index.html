Get in touch

Questions, corrections and recording contributions

Have a listening-test idea, a question we should explain or feedback about the site? Musicians, recording engineers and researchers are also welcome to offer recordings, stems or other material they can authorize for educational demonstrations.

Email a question, idea or recording offer

Please describe what you own or have permission to share before sending large files.

Audio businesses and equipment makers

Manufacturers, distributors and audio retailers are welcome to get in touch about equipment loans, demonstration units, review samples, product information or access to technical measurements. Speakers, amplifiers, DACs and headphones are all relevant to our planned educational comparisons.

Email an equipment or business inquiry

Please include the product, what you are offering, where it would ship from and whether it is a temporary loan or a product to keep. We will discuss shipping, return terms and permissions before accepting anything. An offer does not guarantee coverage, a favourable assessment or editorial approval. Loans, gifts and paid relationships will be disclosed where relevant, and any findings remain independent.

We use email rather than a contact form.
